# Privacy Policy for ADYA

_Last updated: 13 September 2026_

ADYA is a personal task app. This policy describes what it collects, why, and
what you can do about it.

## What ADYA stores on your device

Your lists, tasks, tags, subtasks, reminder times, completion history, theme
choice and app-icon choice are saved locally on your device. If you never sign
in, this data stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

## What ADYA stores in the cloud

If you sign in, your lists, tasks and completion history are synced to Google
Firebase (Cloud Firestore) so they are available on your other devices. Each
account's data is stored in a single private document readable only by that
account.

Signing in with Apple or Google provides ADYA with a user identifier and, where
you permit it, your email address. This is used only to identify your account
for sync. ADYA does not use it for marketing and does not send you email.

Before you sign in, ADYA creates an anonymous account so sync can work without
requiring identity. An anonymous account holds your task data and nothing else.

## Crash reporting

Production builds may send crash and error reports to Sentry to help fix bugs.
These reports contain technical diagnostics — error messages, stack traces, OS
and device model. They are configured to exclude personal information, and
screenshot and screen-content capture are disabled, so your task text is not
included.

## What ADYA does not do

- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
- No analytics or behavioural tracking.
- No selling or sharing of your data with third parties.
- No location collection.
- No access to your contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, ADYA schedules reminders locally on your device at
the times you choose. Reminder content does not leave your device.

## Deleting your data

- **Delete your account:** Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes
  your synced data and your account. It cannot be undone.
- **Delete local data only:** delete the app from your device.

## Service providers

- **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Cloud Firestore) — account sign-in and
  sync. See the [Firebase privacy
  documentation](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
- **Sentry** — crash reporting in production builds only. See the [Sentry
  privacy policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/).
- **Apple** / **Google** — sign-in providers, if you choose to use them.

## Children

ADYA is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data
from them.

## Data retention

Cloud data is kept until you delete your account. Local data is kept until you
delete the app.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected in the "last updated" date
above and, where significant, noted in the app's release notes.
